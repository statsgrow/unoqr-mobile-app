# Editable Affinity marketing artwork

Open the SVG sources in Affinity and save in its native format. Text uses the installed Bricolage Grotesque font and remains text. The official UnoQR wordmark is vector artwork. Phone borders, orange shadows, status icons and backgrounds are separate shapes.

The completed feature graphic is 1024 × 500 px. A matching feature graphic with empty phone screens and a standalone 500 × 1024 phone template are included. The phone itself is 430 × 932 units, excluding its optional orange shadow.

For the standalone template, replace the layer named SCREENSHOT AREA - place your screenshot here. Its screen area starts below the status bar and finishes above the home indicator. Crop status/navigation chrome out of your screenshot before fitting it, to avoid duplicate time or camera elements. Keep the frame/status-bar layers above your image.
